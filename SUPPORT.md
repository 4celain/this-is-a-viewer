# Support / 지원

## English

For questions or support with This is a Viewer, contact:

- Email: [thisisapps.support@gmail.com](mailto:thisisapps.support@gmail.com)
- Bug reports: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/4celain/this-is-a-viewer/issues)

When reporting a problem, include the app version, macOS version, reproduction
steps, and expected behavior when possible. Avoid sending images, file names,
local paths, or other private information unless they are essential, and redact
sensitive content before attaching anything.
